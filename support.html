Plenty of Plates — Support

The dinner app that lets picky eaters — and the people who feed them — decide together. Swipe through recipes, build your week, get one grocery list, and cook with step-by-step mode.

How it works

  • Discover: swipe right to add a recipe to your week, left to skip, up to favorite. Tap the expand icon for a full-screen swipe view.
  • Plan: your week's lineup lives here. Tap "Optimize My Week" to favor recipes that share ingredients.
  • Grocery: one aisle-grouped list built from your lineup, minus what your pantry already has.
  • Cook Mode: step-by-step instructions with timers. Your screen stays awake while you cook.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-in. Everything stays on your phone.

Does it work offline?
Planning, grocery lists, and cook mode work offline. Recipe photos need an internet connection the first time they load.

How do notifications work?
Reminders are scheduled on your device only. Turn them off in iOS Settings, or in the app under Profile.

How do I delete my data?
Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Where do the recipes come from?
Original Plenty of Plates recipes, TheMealDB, and the Wikibooks Cookbook (adapted under CC BY-SA 4.0, credited on each recipe page).
