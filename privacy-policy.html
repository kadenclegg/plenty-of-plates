Privacy Policy — Plenty of Plates

Effective October 27, 2026. Version 1.0.0.

Short version: Plenty of Plates does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything you do in the app — your week plan, pantry, favorites, and cook history — stays on your phone.

Information we collect

None. Plenty of Plates has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal information, and we do not receive any data from your device.

Information stored on your device

Your meal plans, pantry items, favorite recipes, and cooking history are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We cannot see, access, or recover it.

Recipe photos from third parties

Recipe photos load from third-party hosts (Unsplash, TheMealDB, and Wikimedia Commons). When a photo loads, those services may receive your device's IP address and standard browser/device information, just as any website does when it serves an image. Plenty of Plates itself does not send them any personal information, and we do not use any data from those requests.

Notifications

Reminders (such as the dinner nudge and weekly lineup reset) are scheduled locally on your device. They do not go through any server. You can turn them off anytime in iOS Settings.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
